The capture runs without narration. In order, it moves through:

- **Image channels** — the morphology image under multi-channel display, with
  per-channel intensity thresholds and gamma, and the H&E image toggled in
  over the top.
- **Region selection** — freehand and rectangular tools drawing a boundary
  around a structure, with the coordinates of a previous selection available
  to paste back in.
- **Gene search** — the panel listing every gene in the assay against its
  count and expression value.
- **Transcript inspection** — individual transcripts resolved at zoom, each
  one addressable for its ID, Q-score and position in the section.
